# 5. Expected Outcomes

**Project:** EggTrace AI: Egg Batch Traceability and Quality Record Management
**Team:** Code Warriors

---

## 5.1 Prototype Deliverables

- A working web application with batch creation, inspection and grading, and storage and movement history.
- QR label generation and **scan-based handover** recording.
- A **public consumer page** reached by scanning the QR code on the carton.
- A **rapid recall tool** that lists all affected buyers for a batch.
- A demo dataset and a live deployment.
- Phase 2 prototypes: crack detection from images, shelf-life estimation, supplier scorecards.

## 5.2 Measurable Impact

| Metric | Current (manual) | With EggTrace AI |
|---|---|---|
| Time to trace a batch to all buyers | Hours to days | **Under 5 seconds** |
| Handover records captured | Partial / paper | **100% digital (QR scan)** |
| Recall scope | Whole lots discarded | **Only affected batches** |
| Inspection data availability | Paper registers | **Instant, searchable** |
| Defect detection | Manual, subjective | **Automated, consistent** (Phase 2) |
| Consumer visibility | None | **Farm and freshness in one scan** |

## 5.3 Benefits by Stakeholder

- **Farms:** proof of quality, better prices, and scorecards that reward good practice.
- **Collection centres and warehouses:** clear custody records and fewer disputes.
- **Transporters:** proof of delivery and condition at handover.
- **Buyers and retailers:** verified origin and freshness, plus faster, targeted recalls.
- **Consumers:** **trust**, because they can see where and when their eggs were produced.
- **Regulators:** quick audits and FSSAI and export compliance support.

## 5.4 Expected Impact

1. **Faster recalls:** a response measured in seconds instead of days limits health risk.
2. **Higher buyer trust:** transparent farm-to-table information.
3. **Improved quality accountability:** every defect can be linked to a stage and a party.
4. **Less waste:** precise recalls and shelf-life alerts reduce spoilage and over-recall.

## 5.5 Future Scope

- IoT temperature sensors that log cold-chain data automatically.
- Blockchain anchoring for tamper-proof audit trails.
- Multilingual mobile app (Tamil, Hindi, Telugu) for farm workers.
- Integration with FSSAI and the export certification systems of the Agricultural and Processed Food Products Export Development Authority (APEDA).
- Extension to other perishables (dairy, meat, produce).
